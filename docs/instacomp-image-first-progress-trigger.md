# InstaComp Image-First Competition and Progress

- Listing images are the evidence of record; seller titles are claims to verify.
- A visually exact card may remain active competition when its seller title names the wrong parallel, but it is flagged `seller title mislabeled`.
- Wrong or inconclusive images remain rejected and never affect pricing.
- Sold listings alone calculate suggested price; active listings show competition only.
- Scans display percentage, current card, elapsed time and an animated activity bar.
